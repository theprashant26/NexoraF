Recognition, terms, and privacy.

The statements below describe the scope of the information published on this website.

Recognition and affiliation

Nexora Institute publishes information about its programmes, certificates and institutional activities based on its applicable academic and administrative structure.

Any claim regarding government approval, university affiliation, accreditation, statutory recognition, professional licensing or third-party certification will be published only where the applicable approval, affiliation, recognition or documentary evidence exists.

Unless specifically stated otherwise, certificates and diplomas issued by Nexora Institute are institute-level professional training credentials and should not be represented as government, university, UGC, AICTE, statutory-board or professional-licensing qualifications without the applicable approval, affiliation or recognition.

Certificates

Upon successful completion of the prescribed programme and fulfilment of applicable academic requirements, eligible learners may be awarded an institute-level professional training certificate by Nexora Institute. The certificate will state the relevant programme name, programme code and other applicable certificate details.

Any external affiliation, accreditation, recognition or certification will only be published where the Institute holds valid documentary evidence supporting that specific claim.

Career and employment

Nexora Institute's programmes are designed to provide professional knowledge, practical skills and career-oriented training.

Completion of a programme does not by itself guarantee employment, appointment or a specific salary.

Career opportunities may depend on the learner's qualification, skills, experience, employer requirements, selection process and prevailing employment conditions. Where a specific placement or employment arrangement exists, its applicable terms and conditions will be communicated separately.

  • Admissions

  • Communication

Terms and conditions

By accessing the Nexora Institute website or applying for a programme, users agree to comply with the applicable website and programme terms.

  1. Programme information is subject to change where necessary.
  2. Eligibility requirements vary by programme.
  3. Admission is subject to verification and acceptance by the Institute.
  4. Students must provide accurate information and genuine documents.
  5. Fees must be paid according to the applicable payment schedule.
  6. Certificates are issued subject to fulfilment of programme requirements.
  7. The Institute does not guarantee employment, appointment or a specific salary merely because a learner completes a programme.
  8. Any placement support, where offered, is subject to applicable terms.
  9. The Institute may update programme schedules, faculty assignments, course content or administrative procedures when reasonably necessary.
  10. Students must comply with applicable academic, disciplinary and administrative requirements.

Refund and cancellation

Fee payment

Students should review the applicable programme fee and payment terms before completing enrolment. All payments should be made through authorised payment channels of Nexora Institute, and students should retain the payment receipt or acknowledgement for their records.

Cancellation

A cancellation request must be submitted through the prescribed official communication channel. Cancellation eligibility and applicable deductions will depend on:

  • Date of cancellation request
  • Services already provided
  • Registration status
  • Study material issued
  • Whether training has commenced
  • Assessment or examination arrangements
  • Third-party charges, where applicable

Refund

Where a refund is approved, the eligible amount will be processed according to the applicable refund policy. Certain fees may be non-refundable where the relevant service has already been provided or costs have already been incurred.

Refund processing time: 5–7 working days from the date of approval. The final refund terms applicable to each programme will be communicated before enrolment.

Programme transfer

Students may request a transfer from one programme to another, subject to programme availability, eligibility requirements, batch availability, academic status, fee difference and administrative approval.

A transfer request must be submitted to the Admissions or Academic Department. If the new programme has a higher fee, the applicable difference may be payable. Any adjustment relating to a lower-fee programme will be governed by the applicable fee and refund terms.
