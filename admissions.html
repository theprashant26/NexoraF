Eligibility

Applicants must hold an Intermediate (Class 12) qualification or above. Programmes run for three to six months and are delivered online.

The eligibility shown on each programme page is the one that applies to it.

Documents needed

  • 01Recent passport-size photographs
  • 02Aadhaar Card or valid identity proof
  • 03PAN Card, where applicable
  • 04Class 10 marksheet or certificate
  • 05Class 12 marksheet or certificate
  • 06Graduation marksheet or degree certificate, where applicable
  • 07Previous qualification certificates
  • 08Address proof, where required
  • 09Programme-specific documents
  • 10Any additional document required for verification

All documents submitted must be genuine and valid. Submission of forged, false or misleading documents may result in cancellation of admission.

Fees, duration, eligibility, and documents should be confirmed with the institute before enrolment.

Fees and duration vary by programme, and the figures for each one are shown on its programme page. The exact fee structure and payment terms are confirmed before enrolment.

The programme fee may include

  • Training sessions
  • Academic guidance
  • Course or study material, where applicable
  • Internal assessments
  • Learner support
  • Course completion certificate, subject to applicable requirements

The programme fee does not include

  • Travel expenses
  • Accommodation
  • Food and personal expenses
  • Government or statutory charges, where applicable
  • Third-party examination or certification charges
  • Any service specifically excluded from the programme

Every programme is offered at three levels — a 3-month basic certificate, a 6-month advanced certificate, and a one-year professional diploma. Each level has its own fee, shown on the programme's own page.

The 3-month programmes are payable in three instalments: the first at the time of registration, and the balance in two further monthly payments. The first instalment forms part of the total programme fee — it is not an additional charge. The 6-month and one-year programmes are paid by EMI on request — contact the Admissions Department and they will set up a schedule.
